Starting Role
Where does your career begin? You'll grow toward Head Coach regardless of your starting point. Coordinators get a lower-pressure environment to develop their identity before taking the reins of a full program.
🏟️
Head Coach
Head Coach
Full control from day one. You manage recruiting, staff, NIL, and game planning. You can call offense, defense, both, or neither — your choice on any given game. Higher expectations and more pressure, but the complete experience immediately.
⚔️
Coordinator
Offensive Coordinator
Build your reputation on offense. Lower pressure, develop your play calling identity. The OC decision tree unlocks paths toward becoming a head coach. You call the offense every game regardless of your eventual play calling preference.
🛡️
Coordinator
Defensive Coordinator
Build your reputation on defense. The DC decision tree leads toward HC. You call the defense every game. A strong defensive identity here becomes your calling card as you move up the coaching ladder.
Starting Skill Tree
Pick one area to specialize in at the start of your career. You'll unlock all trees over time — this gives you a meaningful head start in one direction. The Championship and NIL trees unlock through career achievements.
